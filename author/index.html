The maintainer

The person who builds and maintains Editloft

Editloft is a free plugin that adds an editorial calendar, an audit trail and role-based publishing rules to WordPress. I build and maintain it.

It is a fork of an existing editorial plugin, Edit Flow, brought up to date for current WordPress, and I say plainly where it came from. The about page tells the story.

Why you can trust me on this

  • I have spoken at more than 18 WordCamps, the community events where WordPress people share what works.
  • I have been in WordPress since 2007, with dozens of plugins built and released, five of them on WordPress.org.
  • I have been building on the web since 1996, including a lot of publishing work.
  • I publish on WordPress myself, so I meet the rough edges of an editorial workflow before you do.
