Accessibility

Accessibility

I want this site and the plugin to work for everyone. Here is what I aim for, what I have checked, and what I have not.

The website

I build editloft.com aiming for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA, the widely used standard for accessible websites. In plain terms, the aim is that you can get around with a keyboard, a screen reader can read the pages, zooming in does not break them, and the text is easy to see.

  • Pages use real headings, lists, links and buttons, so assistive technology can follow the structure.
  • Anything you can click can also be reached and used from the keyboard.
  • Images that carry information have text descriptions.
  • I do not rely on colour alone to show anything.

The plugin

Release 0.6263.1931 added a keyboard and touch way to do everything the calendar's drag and drop does, an undo for every move, labelled filter controls, screen reader announcements for results and errors, and larger status text with enough contrast. You can read the details in the changelog.

I have not yet audited the plugin against WCAG 2.2 AA. The calendar audit and the published report are open issues on GitHub (#11 and #15), and I will link the report here when it exists.

Where it falls short

This is a small site and a small project, and nobody outside my own work has audited either one for accessibility. I may have missed something.

If something does not work for you

Please tell me. Email hello@editloft.com with the page address, what went wrong and any assistive technology you use. I will reply within a few business days and fix whatever is getting in your way. A problem in the plugin itself is also welcome as a GitHub issue.
